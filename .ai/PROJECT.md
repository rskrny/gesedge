## What this is
The official website for Global Edge Strategies (GES LLC, Wyoming) and its Chinese
counterpart, Chengdu Huanqiao (HuanQiao WFOE) — currently live at gesedge.com on a
Next.js stack. This project is a **complete redesign and rebuild**: the existing site
is a useful reference, but most of its code is not expected to carry over. Backend,
hosting, and CMS are all being replaced.

## Goals
A fully redesigned, modernized website for the combined GES / Chengdu Huanqiao brand,
built on a new backend, new hosting, and a new CMS. Success = a site that credibly
represents the business to cross-border (US↔China) clients, is easy for the team to
update through the CMS, and replaces the current build. Specific design direction and
stack choices are still open (tracked in STATE.md / DECISIONS.md).

## Operator / audience
- **Operators:** Ryan Kearney (owner/operator) and Shiying (developer).
- **Audience:** Prospective clients of GES (US SMBs needing custom software/automation)
  and Chengdu Huanqiao (companies operating between the US and China — market entry,
  compliance, cross-border tech). Bilingual EN/ZH.

## Constraints
Constraints are in flux for this rebuild and will firm up as decisions are made:
- **Stack (changing):** new backend, new hosting, new CMS — all to be selected. The
  current Next.js / Vercel / Supabase stack is NOT a constraint; it is being replaced.
- **Design (open):** full redesign. The current dark-charcoal / salmon theme and fonts
  are not locked.
- **Persisting:** anti-AI-slop writing rules (see AGENTS.md); free / low-cost tooling
  preference; surgical, reviewed changes; never deploy to production without approval;
  never fabricate testimonials, reviews, or metrics.
